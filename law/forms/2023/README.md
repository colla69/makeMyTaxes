## Differences from the forms of 2022

- All forms: the fields keep their meaning, but most line numbers change. `Definition2023` holds
  the lines of 2023.
- Anlage N, home office: 2022 has one line, 45, for days of work only at home. 2023 has line 61 for
  days with another workplace and line 62 for days without one. The source is § 4(5) sentence 1
  no. 6c sentences 1 and 2 EStG, which § 52(6) sentence 12 EStG applies from 2023.
- Anlage N, way to work: 2022 has the days, the kilometres and the transport on line 35. 2023 has
  the days on line 32 and the kilometres on line 33. The kilometres go again on line 34 for a car,
  on line 35 for a collective transport by the employer, or on line 36 for other transport. The
  form has no field for the transport itself (2023AnlN032NET).
